Architecture & Design
Branding

Apex Collective

From Microsoft Word logo to Series-A aesthetic — in 14 days.

The Situation

Apex Collective had been operating for three years with a brand identity assembled from free assets and gut feeling. They had a genuine reputation in their market — but their visual presence suggested otherwise. Every time a prospect Googled them before a meeting, the brand was doing active damage.

What We Built

We delivered a complete brand identity system over 14 days: primary and secondary logo marks, full color system, typography hierarchy, brand voice document, business collateral, and a 60-page brand standards guide. Every asset was built for scalability — from a favicon to a construction hoarding.

The Outcome

Within 60 days of launch, inbound inquiries tripled. The team reported that prospects began referencing the brand in conversations — a first. Two enterprise clients specifically cited the brand's professional appearance as a deciding factor in their selection process.

Real Estate
Web Design SEO

Lux Realty Group

62% fewer bounces. 4× more leads. Same market, different brand.

The Situation

Lux Realty was generating traffic through referrals and word of mouth — but their website was converting at a dismal 1.2%. The design was dated, the mobile experience was broken, and there was no clear path for a visitor to take action. Good leads were landing and immediately leaving.

What We Built

Complete custom website redesign and development — mobile-first, built for speed, engineered around conversion. We rebuilt the information architecture from scratch, created dedicated landing pages for each property tier, integrated a lead capture funnel, and optimized every page for local SEO. Launched in 3 weeks with full handoff documentation.

The Outcome

Bounce rate fell from 78% to 16% within the first month. Monthly qualified leads increased 4×. The site now ranks on page one of Google for 14 high-intent local keywords. The team closed their biggest-ever deal through a cold web lead six weeks after launch.

Health & Fitness
Social Media

Nova Fitness

1,200 followers. Zero strategy. 90 days later: sold out.

The Situation

Nova Fitness had a genuinely exceptional product — a boutique studio with elite coaches and real community culture. Their problem wasn't the gym. It was that nobody outside their zip code knew it existed. 1,200 Instagram followers. Zero TikTok presence. No content strategy. No one managing their community.

What We Did

Full social media takeover: Instagram + TikTok. We developed a content strategy built on three pillars — transformation stories, community moments, and educational content. Produced all creative in-house including Reels, carousels, and graphics. Managed posting schedules, hashtag strategy, comment moderation, and DM pipeline 7 days a week.

The Outcome

40,000 new followers across platforms in 90 days. Engagement rate increased 347%. The studio went from struggling to fill off-peak slots to maintaining a waitlist — something the owners said they "didn't know was possible for a gym." Four corporate wellness partnerships were initiated through social media DMs alone.

Paid Ads Branding

Vault Financial

4.8× ROAS. $340K attributed Q1 revenue. Every dollar tracked.

The Situation

Vault Financial was running ads in-house with a 1.2× ROAS — essentially breaking even after platform fees. Their ad creative looked like a compliance document. Their targeting was broad, their copy was generic, and their landing pages had no conversion architecture. They were paying to generate awareness for leads their competitors closed.

What We Did

We rebuilt their paid media operation from scratch — strategy, creative, and management. New brand visual identity for ad creative first, then full campaign architecture across Meta and Google: cold audience prospecting, warm retargeting, and a conversion-optimized landing page for each offer. All creative produced in-house. Weekly reporting with actual revenue attribution.

The Outcome

ROAS jumped from 1.2× to 4.8× within the first 60 days. Q1 revenue directly attributed to the ad campaigns: $340,000. Cost-per-qualified-lead dropped 71%. The team paused all other marketing channels to reallocate budget into the paid campaigns because nothing else came close to the performance.

05
Food & Beverage
Video Production Social Media

Meridian Coffee

One brand film. $180K in attributed revenue. 2.4M views. Baristas crying.

The Situation

Meridian was a premium single-origin coffee roaster with a product and a supply chain story that deserved to be seen. Their problem was invisibility — competing on price against brands with inferior coffee but bigger marketing budgets. Their social media was product photos and discount codes. Their story was untold.

What We Made

A three-minute brand film — shot across two days, tracing the journey from farm to cup with a cinematic treatment that made a local roaster feel like an internationally recognized brand. We simultaneously took over their social channels, built a content strategy around the film release, and produced a 60-second cut and four 15-second cuts for ads and social.

The Outcome

The brand film generated 2.4M views across platforms organically in the first 90 days. $180,000 in directly attributed revenue from customers who cited the film. Eight new wholesale accounts were won — buyers who reached out after seeing the video. The 15-second social cuts ran as paid ads and delivered a 3.6× ROAS with zero audience targeting changes.

$180K Attributed revenue From brand film alone
2.4M Organic views First 90 days
8 New wholesale accounts Sourced via film
3.6× ROAS on video ads 15-sec social cuts
