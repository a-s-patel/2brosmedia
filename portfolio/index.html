ARCHITECTURE
FIRM Brand Identity
Architecture & Design Branding

Architecture Firm

The Challenge

The firm had been operating for three years with a logo made in Microsoft Word and zero visual consistency across any of their materials. They were winning work on reputation alone — but losing deals to better-looking competitors they knew were inferior. They needed a brand that matched their actual caliber.

What We Built

A complete brand identity system — positioning, naming architecture, logo suite, color palette, typography, brand voice, and a 60-page guidelines document. We also redesigned all client-facing collateral including proposals, presentations, and social assets.

280% Brand recognition lift in 60 days
3× Increase in inbound inquiries
14 days Full delivery including revisions
BOUTIQUE
FITNESS Social Media
Health & Fitness Social Media Management

Boutique Fitness Studio

The Challenge

A boutique fitness studio with exceptional coaches and real community energy — but their Instagram lacked strategy, consistency, and reach. Competitors had 10× the marketing budget. They needed to outperform them organically.

What We Did

Full social media takeover across Instagram and TikTok. We built a content strategy around transformation stories, community highlights, and educational fitness content — producing all creative in-house with daily posting, community management, and DM handling.

347% Engagement rate increase
40K New followers in 90 days
60 days Studio fully sold out / waitlist
FERTILITY
TECH Performance Marketing
Health & Wellness Paid Ads

Fertility Tech Company

The Challenge

Launching a fertility tracking bracelet in Canada with strong demand but no structured paid acquisition strategy. CPAs ranged from $87 to $1,200+ across channels. They needed to hit $330K Q1 revenue and scale 3× — with no clear playbook for how to get there.

What We Built

A 6-month acquisition plan across email, paid social, SEM, programmatic, influencer, and content. Segmented lifecycle email flows, multi-channel budget allocation, a test-and-scale framework, and creative pattern identification to pause high-CPA campaigns and double down on winners.

$1.3M Revenue in 9 months
4,000 Bracelets sold
$150–180 CPA across top channels
