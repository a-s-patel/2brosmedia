40,000 new followers across platforms in 90 days. Engagement rate increased 347%. The studio went from struggling to fill off-peak slots to maintaining a full waitlist. Four corporate wellness partnerships were initiated through social media DMs alone — something that had never happened before we took over.

Health & Wellness
Performance Marketing

Fertility Tech Company

CPAs from $1,200 down to $150. $1.3M revenue. 9 months.

The Situation

A fertility tracking bracelet launching in Canada with strong demand but no structured paid acquisition strategy. CPAs ranged from $87 to $1,200+ depending on the channel — with no framework to understand why. They needed to hit $330K Q1 revenue and scale 3× without a clear playbook for how to get there.

What We Built

A 6-month acquisition plan across paid social, SEM, programmatic, email, influencer, and content. Segmented lifecycle email flows, multi-channel budget allocation with weekly rebalancing, and a test-and-scale framework that identified winning creative patterns and paused high-CPA campaigns systematically rather than reactively.

The Outcome

$1.3M in revenue over 9 months — nearly 4× the original Q1 target. 4,000 bracelets sold. CPAs stabilized between $150–$180 across top-performing channels, down from $1,200+ on the worst performers that were cut in the first audit. The acquisition system continued generating return after the initial engagement closed.

Consumer Apps
User Acquisition Strategy

Family Safety App

1M installs. 33% paid-to-subscription. $5 CPI with $10 LTV.

The Situation

The app needed to scale from 1.3M to 2.6M installs while maintaining install quality. Retention varied dramatically across sources — affiliates were delivering 40% D1 retention versus 55% organic — and the team lacked the creative strategy to attract high-value users at scale across different audience segments.

What We Built

A full UA strategy across DSPs, preloads, and clean traffic sources. We developed persona-driven creative for three distinct segments — moms, dads, and caregivers — with tailored messaging for each. Implemented daypart targeting and multi-variant testing frameworks tied directly to D1 and D7 retention metrics, not just install volume.

The Outcome

1M installs delivered with a 33% paid-to-subscription conversion rate — significantly above category benchmarks. CPI held at $5 against a $10 LTV, producing a 2× ROAS. The persona-based creative strategy improved retention metrics across all traffic sources by giving the algorithm higher-quality engagement signals to optimize against.

0% Paid-to-subscription Conversion rate
$5 CPI achieved vs. $10 LTV = 2× ROAS
3 Persona segments built Moms, dads, caregivers

Gaming
Growth Strategy Performance Marketing

PC Gaming Developer

56% blended ROAS on $1M monthly. Cross-device attribution solved.

The Situation

A desktop gaming developer needed to shift from 78% ROAS efficiency down to 50% ROAS to unlock 3–10× more scale. The core challenge: advertising a desktop-only product through mobile ad inventory with no cross-device tracking infrastructure. Scale was available but structurally inaccessible.

What We Built

A dual-budget strategy allocating $1M monthly across ROAS-optimized and growth-oriented campaigns. We designed the mobile-to-desktop conversion infrastructure, built A/B test evaluation frameworks, and implemented a creative strategy and persona research system that could scale across multiple game titles simultaneously.

The Outcome

Achieved 56% blended ROAS across $1M monthly spend — beating the 50% target and unlocking the scale headroom the team needed. Cross-device attribution was fully enabled for the first time, giving the growth team visibility they had never had. Creative operations scaled across multiple game titles without proportional increases in overhead.
