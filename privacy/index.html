Privacy Policy

2Bros Media LLC · 2bros.click

We built this site to make brands profitable, not to hoard your data. Here's exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it — no legalese buried in fine print you'll never read.

Info We Collect How We Use It Cookies & Analytics Sharing Your Info Data Retention Your Rights Security Children's Privacy Changes to This Policy Contact Us

Information We Collect

When you use 2bros.click, we collect information in a few ways:

  • Information you give us directly — your name, email, business or brand name, project details, and budget range when you submit our contact form.
  • Automatic technical data — IP address, browser type, device type, referring URL, pages visited, and time spent on the site, collected through analytics tools.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — small data files used to understand how visitors use the site and to measure the performance of our marketing.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your consultation request and follow up about your project
  • Understand how people find and use our site so we can improve it
  • Measure the performance of our own marketing campaigns
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of the site
  • Comply with legal obligations where applicable

We do not sell your personal information. Full stop.

Cookies & Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand traffic and site performance, including which pages people visit and which actions — like submitting our contact form — count as key conversions. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect this data. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings to block third-party cookies.

We also use Google Search Console to monitor how our site appears in search results. This does not collect personal information about individual visitors.

Sharing Your Information

We share information only with the service providers that help us run this site and respond to inquiries:

  • Formspree — processes and delivers the contact form submissions you send us
  • Google — provides our analytics and search performance tools
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website

Each of these providers has its own privacy practices governing the data that passes through their systems. We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users or the public.

Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain business records, and no longer than needed for those purposes. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics' standard retention settings.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, reach out using the contact details below and we'll handle it directly — no runaround.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect, including using trusted third-party providers with their own security practices. That said, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our site and services are intended for businesses and individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal or operational reasons. The "Effective" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version. Continued use of the site after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out through our contact page and we'll get back to you within 24 hours — same as we promise everyone else.
